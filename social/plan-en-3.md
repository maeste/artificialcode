# Plan EN 3: Alternating Platforms (7 Days)
## LINCE Social Campaign

---

## Day 1 (Monday) - LinkedIn EN (L4c)
### Launch Announcement

> "Today we're open-sourcing LINCE, and I want to tell you why it exists."

**Rationale:** Clean, direct launch announcement on LinkedIn to start the week. The "why it exists" framing invites the audience into the story behind the product.

---

## Day 2 (Tuesday) - X.com (X1a)
### Technical Depth

> "I run AI coding agents with zero permission prompts. No approvals, no interruptions, no anxiety."

**Rationale:** After the LinkedIn launch, X.com gets the technical deep-dive on the sandbox. The repetition of "no" creates a rhythmic, memorable hook.

---

## Day 3 (Wednesday) - LinkedIn EN (L1b)
### Security Angle

> "'Are you sure you want to allow this action?' If you use AI coding agents, you've clicked that dialog hundreds of times."

**Rationale:** Relatable pain point for the LinkedIn professional audience. Mid-week timing catches people reflecting on their own tool frustrations.

---

## Day 4 (Thursday) - X.com (X2a)
### Productivity Hook

> "I run 5 AI coding agents simultaneously. One refactors, one writes tests, one fixes CI..."

**Rationale:** Numbers-driven post that paints a vivid picture. Thursday X.com audiences engage well with concrete, tangible productivity claims.

---

## Day 5 (Friday) - LinkedIn EN (L2c)
### Forward-Looking

> "What if the real productivity gain from AI agents isn't faster code generation, but parallel code generation?"

**Rationale:** Friday is ideal for reflective, forward-looking content on LinkedIn. The question format invites thoughtful comments heading into the weekend.

---

## Day 6 (Saturday) - X.com (X3c)
### Weekend Debate

> "What if the next evolution of developer tools isn't adding more features to the IDE, but removing the IDE entirely?"

**Rationale:** Weekend X.com thrives on debate-worthy questions. This challenges the IDE paradigm and sparks threads that carry into the following week.

---

## Day 7 (Sunday) - LinkedIn EN (L3a)
### Bold Personal Story

> "I deleted my IDE. Then my browser. Then every extra app I used for coding."

**Rationale:** Sunday LinkedIn readers enjoy longer, personal narrative content. The dramatic, sequential revelation creates a compelling read before the work week begins.
