# Plan EN 2: Story-First (7 Days)
## LINCE Social Campaign

---

## Day 1 (Monday) - Dual Platform Launch

### LinkedIn EN (L4a) - Founder Story
> "A couple of months ago, I stood on stage at Voxxed Day Ticino and said: 'The attack surface is you, not your software.'"

**Rationale:** Opens the campaign with a personal founder narrative on LinkedIn. Story-driven content builds trust and emotional connection with a professional audience.

### X.com (X4c) - Story With Punch
> "'The attack surface is you, not your software.' I said this at a conference a couple of months ago. Then we spent the following weeks building a tool based on that idea."

**Rationale:** Mirrors the LinkedIn story but with a punchier, more direct tone for X.com. Dual-platform launch maximizes Day 1 reach.

---

## Day 2 (Tuesday) - X.com (X1b)
### Core Innovation After Story

> "'Are you sure you want to allow this action?' ...you've seen this dialog 10,000 times."

**Rationale:** After establishing the origin story, introduce the core innovation (sandbox security). The relatable frustration hooks developers who lived through permission fatigue.

---

## Day 3 (Wednesday) - LinkedIn EN (L1c)
### Deepen Security Narrative

> "The most dangerous moment in AI-assisted coding isn't when the agent makes a mistake."

**Rationale:** Deepens the security story arc with a provocative, incomplete statement that compels clicks and comments. Wednesday LinkedIn engagement is strong.

---

## Day 4 (Thursday) - X.com (X2c)
### Provocative Multi-Agent Take

> "Hot take: running one AI coding agent at a time is like having a team of 5 engineers and only letting one work."

**Rationale:** Shifts from security to multi-agent productivity with a deliberately provocative analogy. "Hot take" framing drives engagement on X.com.

---

## Day 5 (Friday) - LinkedIn EN (L2a)
### Professional Multi-Agent Pitch

> "Running one AI coding agent is powerful. Running five at the same time is chaos."

**Rationale:** Professional, balanced framing of the multi-agent challenge for LinkedIn. Sets up LINCE as the solution to a problem the audience recognizes.

---

## Day 7 (Sunday) - X.com (X3b)
### Weekend Controversial Take

> "Controversial opinion: your IDE is slowing you down."

**Rationale:** Weekend content on X.com benefits from bold, debate-sparking statements. This challenges a deeply held belief, driving replies and quote tweets into Monday.
