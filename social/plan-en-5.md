# Plan EN 5: Feature Spotlight (14 Days)
## LINCE Social Campaign

---

## Day 1 (Monday) - Dual Platform Launch

### LinkedIn EN (L4c) - Comprehensive Launch
> "Today we're open-sourcing LINCE, and I want to tell you why it exists."

**Rationale:** Comprehensive launch announcement on LinkedIn. The "why" framing sets up the feature spotlight structure for the rest of the campaign.

### X.com (X4b) - Story Angle Launch
> "We spent the last few weeks building a tool that makes AI coding agents actually usable. Today we're releasing it for free."

**Rationale:** Story-driven launch on X.com emphasizing the free/open-source angle. Creates a different entry point from the LinkedIn post.

---

## Day 2 (Tuesday) - X.com (X1a)
### Sandbox Spotlight

> "I run AI coding agents with zero permission prompts. No approvals, no interruptions, no anxiety."

**Rationale:** First feature spotlight: the sandbox. The triple "no" structure is punchy and memorable for X.com's fast-scrolling audience.

---

## Day 3 (Wednesday) - LinkedIn EN (L1c)
### Sandbox Business Case

> "The most dangerous moment in AI-assisted coding isn't when the agent makes a mistake."

**Rationale:** Deepens the sandbox spotlight with a business-case framing for LinkedIn professionals. The incomplete statement compels engagement.

---

## Day 5 (Friday) - X.com (X2b)
### Multi-Agent Spotlight

> "The way most developers use AI agents: prompt one agent, wait, review, prompt again. Linear. Slow."

**Rationale:** Second feature spotlight: multi-agent orchestration. The staccato rhythm ("Linear. Slow.") mirrors the frustration being described.

---

## Day 6 (Saturday) - LinkedIn EN (L2a)
### Multi-Agent Leadership

> "Running one AI coding agent is powerful. Running five at the same time is chaos."

**Rationale:** Continues the multi-agent spotlight with a leadership-oriented framing. Saturday LinkedIn catches weekend readers with more reflective content.

---

## Day 8 (Monday) - X.com (X3b)
### Terminal Spotlight

> "Controversial opinion: your IDE is slowing you down."

**Rationale:** Third feature spotlight: terminal-native architecture. Week 2 opens with a bold claim that re-energizes the conversation on X.com.

---

## Day 9 (Tuesday) - LinkedIn EN (L3c)
### Terminal Depth

> "The best developer tool is the one you already have: your terminal."

**Rationale:** Continues the terminal spotlight with a positive, practical framing for LinkedIn. Positions LINCE as leveraging what developers already know.

---

## Day 10 (Wednesday) - X.com (X1c)
### Resurface Sandbox

> "At Voxxed Day Ticino I told a room full of developers: 'The attack surface is you, not your software.'"

**Rationale:** Returns to the sandbox spotlight from the conference authority angle. New followers from Week 1 get the origin story context.

---

## Day 11 (Thursday) - LinkedIn EN (L2b)
### Revisit Multi-Agent

> "The biggest lie about AI coding assistants: 'Just use one at a time.'"

**Rationale:** Revisits multi-agent with a myth-busting angle. Thursday LinkedIn engagement supports debate-oriented content.

---

## Day 12 (Friday) - X.com (X2c)
### Provocation

> "Hot take: running one AI coding agent at a time is like having a team of 5 engineers and only letting one work."

**Rationale:** Provocative multi-agent analogy to drive Friday engagement on X.com. "Hot take" framing signals the post is meant to spark discussion.

---

## Day 13 (Saturday) - X.com (X3a)
### Minimal Weekend

> "My entire coding setup: one terminal window. No IDE. No browser. No Electron apps."

**Rationale:** Minimalist terminal post for the weekend. The simplicity of the message matches the simplicity of the tool philosophy.

---

## Day 14 (Sunday) - LinkedIn EN (L4a)
### Full Circle Story

> "A couple of months ago, I stood on stage at Voxxed Day Ticino and said: 'The attack surface is you, not your software.'"

**Rationale:** Closes the 14-day campaign by returning to the origin story. Creates a satisfying narrative circle from launch announcement back to founding motivation.
