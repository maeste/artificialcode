# Plan EN 1: Security-First (7 Days)
## LINCE Social Campaign

---

## Day 1 (Monday) - Dual Platform Launch

### X.com (X1c) - Conference Authority Hook
> "At Voxxed Day Ticino I told a room full of developers: 'The attack surface is you, not your software.'"

**Rationale:** Provocative, authority-building hook that leverages a real conference moment. High virality potential on X.com to kick off the campaign.

### LinkedIn EN (L1a) - Personal Experience Hook
> "I let AI agents run wild on my codebase last night. No permission prompts. No anxiety."

**Rationale:** Strong, curiosity-driven opening on LinkedIn. Different angle from the X.com post, so cross-platform audiences get unique value.

---

## Day 2 (Tuesday) - X.com (X2a)
### Productivity After Security

> "I run 5 AI coding agents simultaneously. One refactors, one writes tests, one fixes CI..."

**Rationale:** After establishing the security narrative on Day 1, pivot to the productivity payoff. Shows that security enables scale, not limits it.

---

## Day 3 (Wednesday) - LinkedIn EN (L2b)
### Mid-Week Professional Engagement

> "The biggest lie about AI coding assistants: 'Just use one at a time.'"

**Rationale:** Wednesday is a strong LinkedIn engagement day. A bold, myth-busting statement invites comments and debate from a professional audience.

---

## Day 4 (Thursday) - X.com (X3a)
### Terminal Angle for Tech Audience

> "My entire coding setup: one terminal window. No IDE. No browser. No Electron apps."

**Rationale:** Shifts to the terminal-native differentiator. Thursday tech audiences on X.com respond well to opinionated tooling takes.

---

## Day 5 (Friday) - LinkedIn EN (L3b)
### Friday Thought-Provoker

> "Your coding setup has 47 open tabs, 3 IDE windows, a chat app, and a monitoring dashboard. Mine has a terminal."

**Rationale:** Relatable, visual contrast that invites self-reflection. Friday audiences on LinkedIn engage with lighter, thought-provoking content.

---

## Day 7 (Sunday) - X.com (X4a)
### Sunday Thread for Monday Visibility

> "Today we're open-sourcing LINCE, a terminal-based multi-agent coding workstation."

**Rationale:** Sunday evening threads on X.com get picked up Monday morning. The launch announcement lands when developer attention peaks at the start of the work week.
