# Plan EN 6: Engagement-Optimized (14 Days)
## LINCE Social Campaign

---

## Day 1 (Monday) - LinkedIn EN (L4a)
### Monday 8-10am Peak

> "A couple of months ago, I stood on stage at Voxxed Day Ticino and said: 'The attack surface is you, not your software.'"

**Rationale:** Monday morning is LinkedIn's highest engagement window (8-10am). The founder story captures attention at the start of the professional week.

---

## Day 2 (Tuesday) - X.com (X1c)
### Tuesday Dev Audience Active

> "At Voxxed Day Ticino I told a room full of developers: 'The attack surface is you, not your software.'"

**Rationale:** Tuesday is peak developer activity on X.com. The conference authority hook resonates with the technical audience at their most engaged.

---

## Day 3 (Wednesday) - LinkedIn EN (L1b)
### Second-Best LinkedIn Day

> "'Are you sure you want to allow this action?' If you use AI coding agents, you've clicked that dialog hundreds of times."

**Rationale:** Wednesday is LinkedIn's second-strongest engagement day. The relatable frustration opens a conversation about sandbox security.

---

## Day 4 (Thursday) - X.com (X2a)
### Numbers-Driven Post

> "I run 5 AI coding agents simultaneously. One refactors, one writes tests, one fixes CI..."

**Rationale:** Thursday X.com audiences respond to concrete, numbers-driven content. The specific agent roles make multi-agent orchestration tangible.

---

## Day 5 (Friday) - LinkedIn EN (L3b)
### Friday Thought-Provoker

> "Your coding setup has 47 open tabs, 3 IDE windows, a chat app, and a monitoring dashboard. Mine has a terminal."

**Rationale:** Friday LinkedIn engagement favors lighter, thought-provoking content. The vivid contrast invites self-reflection heading into the weekend.

---

## Day 7 (Sunday) - X.com (X4a)
### Sunday Pre-Week Thread

> "Today we're open-sourcing LINCE, a terminal-based multi-agent coding workstation."

**Rationale:** Sunday evening threads get maximum Monday morning pickup. The launch announcement is timed for peak visibility at the start of Week 2.

---

## Day 8 (Monday) - LinkedIn EN (L2a)
### Fresh Week, Fresh Angle

> "Running one AI coding agent is powerful. Running five at the same time is chaos."

**Rationale:** New week opens with a fresh angle on LinkedIn. The multi-agent theme builds on the Sunday launch while targeting Monday's peak engagement.

---

## Day 9 (Tuesday) - X.com (X3c)
### Controversial Tuesday

> "What if the next evolution of developer tools isn't adding more features to the IDE, but removing the IDE entirely?"

**Rationale:** Tuesday dev audience on X.com is primed for controversial takes. The question format generates threads and quote tweets throughout the day.

---

## Day 10 (Wednesday) - LinkedIn EN (L4b)
### Second Personal Story

> "Every tool I've built started the same way: I got frustrated enough to stop complaining and start coding."

**Rationale:** Mid-week LinkedIn engagement supports a second personal story. Keeps the human narrative alive between technical posts.

---

## Day 11 (Thursday) - X.com (X2c)
### Opinionated Thursday

> "Hot take: running one AI coding agent at a time is like having a team of 5 engineers and only letting one work."

**Rationale:** Thursday X.com thrives on opinionated content. The "hot take" label and vivid analogy are optimized for engagement and quote tweets.

---

## Day 12 (Friday) - LinkedIn EN (L2c)
### Forward-Looking Friday

> "What if the real productivity gain from AI agents isn't faster code generation, but parallel code generation?"

**Rationale:** Friday LinkedIn readers appreciate forward-looking, visionary content. The question format invites reflective weekend engagement.

---

## Day 13 (Saturday) - X.com (X3a)
### Weekend Minimalist

> "My entire coding setup: one terminal window. No IDE. No browser. No Electron apps."

**Rationale:** Weekend X.com audiences enjoy clean, bold statements about tooling. The minimalist message matches the minimalist philosophy.

---

## Day 14 (Sunday) - LinkedIn EN (L1a)
### Strong Closer

> "I let AI agents run wild on my codebase last night. No permission prompts. No anxiety."

**Rationale:** Closes the 14-day campaign with one of the strongest hooks on LinkedIn. Sunday posting ensures it carries into Monday for a final wave of engagement.
