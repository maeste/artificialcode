# Plan EN 4: Narrative Arc (14 Days)
## LINCE Social Campaign

---

## Day 1 (Monday) - Dual Platform Launch

### LinkedIn EN (L4a) - Set the Narrative
> "A couple of months ago, I stood on stage at Voxxed Day Ticino and said: 'The attack surface is you, not your software.'"

**Rationale:** Opens the 14-day arc with the origin story on LinkedIn. Establishes credibility and emotional investment from the start.

### X.com (X4c) - Dual-Platform Launch
> "'The attack surface is you, not your software.' I said this at a conference a couple of months ago. Then we spent the following weeks building a tool based on that idea."

**Rationale:** Same narrative, punchier delivery for X.com. Dual-platform launch creates maximum Day 1 awareness across both audiences.

---

## Day 3 (Wednesday) - LinkedIn EN (L1a)
### Core Differentiator

> "I let AI agents run wild on my codebase last night. No permission prompts. No anxiety."

**Rationale:** After a one-day pause, reintroduce the audience to the core sandbox innovation. Wednesday LinkedIn engagement peaks make this ideal for the key message.

---

## Day 4 (Thursday) - X.com (X1b)
### Security Deep-Dive

> "'Are you sure you want to allow this action?' ...you've seen this dialog 10,000 times."

**Rationale:** Continues the security thread on X.com with a relatable frustration point. The exaggerated number creates shareability.

---

## Day 6 (Saturday) - X.com (X3a)
### Weekend Content

> "My entire coding setup: one terminal window. No IDE. No browser. No Electron apps."

**Rationale:** Weekend X.com audiences enjoy opinionated tooling content. Introduces the terminal-native angle as a palate cleanser between security and multi-agent themes.

---

## Day 7 (Sunday) - LinkedIn EN (L2a)
### Sunday Productivity Read

> "Running one AI coding agent is powerful. Running five at the same time is chaos."

**Rationale:** Sunday LinkedIn readers have time for longer, reflective content. Introduces the multi-agent theme heading into the new week.

---

## Day 8 (Monday) - X.com (X2c)
### Week 2 Provocative Opening

> "Hot take: running one AI coding agent at a time is like having a team of 5 engineers and only letting one work."

**Rationale:** Week 2 opens with a provocative take on X.com. Re-energizes the campaign with a debate-worthy statement that draws new engagement.

---

## Day 9 (Tuesday) - LinkedIn EN (L3b)
### Thought Leadership

> "Your coding setup has 47 open tabs, 3 IDE windows, a chat app, and a monitoring dashboard. Mine has a terminal."

**Rationale:** Establishes the terminal-native philosophy as thought leadership on LinkedIn. The contrast format is highly shareable among professionals.

---

## Day 11 (Thursday) - X.com (X1c)
### Resurface Security

> "At Voxxed Day Ticino I told a room full of developers: 'The attack surface is you, not your software.'"

**Rationale:** Resurfaces the security narrative for new followers gained during the campaign. Thursday X.com placement catches a fresh audience cycle.

---

## Day 12 (Friday) - LinkedIn EN (L4b)
### Circle Back to Story

> "Every tool I've built started the same way: I got frustrated enough to stop complaining and start coding."

**Rationale:** Returns to the personal founder story, creating a satisfying narrative loop. Friday LinkedIn audiences appreciate authentic, reflective content.

---

## Day 13 (Saturday) - X.com (X3c)
### Weekend Debate

> "What if the next evolution of developer tools isn't adding more features to the IDE, but removing the IDE entirely?"

**Rationale:** Weekend debate content on X.com. The question format generates threads and quote tweets that extend visibility into the final day.

---

## Day 14 (Sunday) - LinkedIn EN (L2c)
### Close With Vision

> "What if the real productivity gain from AI agents isn't faster code generation, but parallel code generation?"

**Rationale:** Closes the 14-day arc with a forward-looking vision statement on LinkedIn. Leaves the audience thinking about where LINCE is heading next.
