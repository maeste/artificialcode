# Review Criteria for Deep-Dive Newsletter

In the editorial review (Step 9), check these criteria on the complete assembled document.

## 1. Voice Authenticity
**Check:** The deep-dive reads as if the user wrote it entirely, with consistent voice throughout

**What to verify:**
- Deep-dive maintains the user's characteristic style from previous editions
- No jarring shifts in register between user-written and enriched portions
- Personal experiences and opinions sound natural, not inserted
- Conversational tone is preserved without becoming overly casual or formal

## 2. Title-Content Alignment
**Check:** The title accurately represents the deep-dive content and is compelling

**What to verify:**
- Title reflects the main argument or finding of the deep-dive
- A reader seeing only the title would get an accurate expectation of the content
- Title is specific enough to stand out, not generic AI buzzword soup

## 3. Introduction-Content Coherence
**Check:** The introduction properly frames the deep-dive and connects to the curated links

**What to verify:**
- Introduction creates clear expectations for what follows
- The deep-dive topic is introduced naturally
- The curated links section is acknowledged or hinted at
- Reader understands the issue's structure from the introduction

## 4. Narrative Flow
**Check:** The document reads smoothly from start to finish

**What to verify:**
- Transition from introduction to agenda is natural
- Transition from agenda to deep-dive works (the `---` separator helps, but context matters)
- Deep-dive has internal logical progression
- Transition from deep-dive to curated links feels intentional, not abrupt
- Curated links complement rather than repeat the deep-dive

## 5. Structural Balance
**Check:** Sections are proportionally balanced and well-paced

**What to verify:**
- Deep-dive is clearly the main content (dominant section)
- Introduction is concise, not competing with the deep-dive
- Curated links section has a consistent rhythm (similar POV depth per link)
- No section feels rushed or overextended relative to others

## 6. Link Commentary Quality
**Check:** Each link's personal commentary adds unique value

**What to verify:**
- POVs are genuinely personal, not generic summaries
- Each commentary tells the reader WHY this link matters to the author
- Commentaries do not repeat points already made in the deep-dive
- If factual descriptions are included, they complement rather than duplicate the POV

## 7. Practical Focus
**Check:** Content focuses on practical implications for AI engineers and technical professionals

**What to verify:**
- Deep-dive connects concepts to real-world application
- Personal experiences ground abstract topics
- Reader gains actionable understanding, not just theory

## 8. Formatting Consistency
**Check:** Markdown formatting is consistent and correct throughout

**What to verify:**
- Heading hierarchy is correct (H1 title, H2 sections, H3 subsections/links)
- Exactly 2 horizontal rules separate the 3 main content blocks
- All links are properly formatted and clickable
- No em dashes appear anywhere (use commas or parentheses)
- Consistent use of bold, italics, and list formatting

## Feedback Format

For each criterion not met:
```
**Criterio [N]: [Nome] - NECESSITA MIGLIORAMENTO**

**Problema:** [Descrizione chiara di cosa non funziona]

**Proposta:** [Suggerimento specifico per risolvere]

**Posizione:** [Sezione/paragrafo dove si trova il problema]
```

Wait for user approval before making any changes to the file.

If all criteria are met:
"Revisione editoriale completata. Tutti i criteri sono soddisfatti."
